About Me

I am an M2 student in Advanced Wireless Communication Systems at CentraleSupélec / Université Paris-Saclay, with an academic background in Machine Learning and Data Science.

My main interests lie at the intersection of Telecommunications, Signal Processing and Machine Learning, particularly in the development of intelligent methods for future wireless communication systems.

My current work involves RF source localization from UAV measurements, where I explore localization approaches based on wireless measurements such as RSSI, ToA and TDoA, together with classical estimation, optimization and machine learning approaches.

Alongside my research work, I am strengthening my theoretical and practical foundations in digital communications, signal processing and information theory, with a particular interest in understanding and implementing communication systems and algorithms from first principles.

My long-term objective is to pursue research in telecommunications, contribute to scientific publications in wireless communications and signal processing, and work in industrial R&D on next-generation communication systems.

Current Work

RF Localization

I am currently working on localization of ground RF sources using UAV measurements.

The project investigates localization using different wireless measurements and algorithmic approaches, including:

RSSI measurements

Time of Arrival

Time Difference of Arrival

Classical localization algorithms

Nonlinear optimization

Machine Learning approaches

Real-world wireless measurements

The objective is to study and improve localization techniques for wireless sources in realistic environments.

Signal Processing and Digital Communications

I am currently developing my foundations in signal processing and communication systems through mathematical study and implementation.

Topics include:

Continuous-time and discrete-time signals

Energy and power of signals

Elementary signals

Convolution

Linear systems

Fourier Series

Fourier Transform

Discrete Fourier Transform

FFT

Sampling

Aliasing

Filtering

Digital modulation

Communication channels

OFDM

My objective is to understand these systems mathematically and implement the fundamental building blocks rather than relying only on high-level communication libraries.
